Engineered For Developers

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

No bloated web frameworks wrapped in Chrome. Real native performance designed to stay out of your way.

True Health Services Probe

Never trust a lying "started" state. Nexus Console probes process PIDs and port responsiveness so you know when a service is genuinely operational or silently hung.

Instant Port Killer

Port 3000 or 5432 already in use? Find the culprit PID instantly with lsof/ss probes and terminate it with a single click, with sudo privilege escalation when needed.

Read-Only Dev Doctor

Safely audit cache bloat in VSCode, Claude Code chat history, and Ollama models. Probes only read and never modify without your explicit authorization.

Integrated xterm.js Terminal

Drop right into your system shell without switching contexts or opening separate terminal emulators. Full ANSI color support and low-latency PTY.

Power & Energy Metrics

Monitor your laptop or workstation power draw, battery health percentage, and system load stats while developing on battery.

Air-Gapped & 100% Offline

Zero analytics. Zero user telemetry. Zero external servers. Your development environment stays private to your hardware.
